About Us

Welcome to Strategic Black Awareness – Preservation

Thank you for showing interest in Strategic Black Awareness – Preservation. We are a group of lifelong friends dedicated to making a positive difference in the community we all grew up in.

Our Mission

Strategic Black Awareness – Preservation is dedicated to preserving our community's legacy, empowering future generations through education and mentorship, and strengthening our community through service, leadership, scholarship, and outreach. We honor the foundation laid by those before us while creating opportunities that inspire growth, achievement, and lasting impact.

Our Vision

A thriving community where history is preserved, education is valued, youth are empowered, and every generation is inspired to lead, serve, and succeed.

We proudly offer scholarships to graduating seniors of Taylor High School and actively participate in programs that strengthen and support our community.

Our Commitment to the Community

Strategic Black Awareness is committed to uplifting, educating, mentoring, and empowering our community through service, leadership, and preservation.

We work to support our youth, encourage educational achievement, honor our history, and create opportunities that promote a stronger future for generations to come.
